Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 5, 2026.

What we collect

We collect limited analytics data to understand site performance and improve user experience. This may include page views, clicks on tracked buttons/links, referral source data (such as UTM parameters), and basic technical data (device/browser).

How we use data

We use analytics to measure traffic, detect abuse, and understand which pages and links are useful. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

Cookies / local storage

Our tracking scripts may store an anonymous identifier in browser storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) to distinguish sessions and measure aggregate usage. This is used for internal analytics and reporting.

Third‑party services

When you click outbound links, you are taken to third‑party websites with their own privacy practices. Review their policies before using them. We are not responsible for third‑party tracking or data collection.

Contact form

If you contact us, we process the information you submit (name, email, subject, message) solely to respond to your request and for basic record‑keeping. To deliver contact messages, we use our own authenticated SMTP mailbox on the pipsnetwork.com domain. Your submission is sent to our server-side mail handler and then delivered to our support inbox. We do not use contact messages for unrelated marketing.

Your choices

You can use browser settings to clear storage, block cookies, or disable JavaScript (note: some features may not work). You may also contact us to request deletion of contact messages you sent, where feasible.
